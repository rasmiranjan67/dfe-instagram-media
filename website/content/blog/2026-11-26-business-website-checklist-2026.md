**A business website in 2026 needs to do three jobs: load fast on a phone, be found on Google and AI search, and make contacting you effortless.** If it misses any one of these, visitors leave and go to a competitor. Use this checklist to see where your site stands.

## 1. A headline that says what you do and where

Visitors decide in seconds whether they're in the right place. Your first line should name your service and your area, for example "Website and app development in Bhubaneswar", not a vague slogan.

## 2. A WhatsApp button on every page

Many customers in India would rather send a quick WhatsApp message than fill in a form. Add a WhatsApp button that stays visible as people scroll, with a pre-filled message so they only need to press send.

## 3. Fast loading on mobile

Many visitors arrive on a phone, often on mobile data. Compress images, avoid heavy sliders and test your site with Google's free PageSpeed Insights tool. Aim for the page to be usable within a couple of seconds.

## 4. One page for each service

A single "Services" list makes it hard for Google to match you to specific searches. Give each main service its own page with its own title, description and FAQs, so you can rank for "website development", "app development" and so on separately.

## 5. Clear, honest proof

Show real work: screenshots of projects, client logos you have permission to use, and genuine Google reviews. Never invent testimonials or numbers. Fake proof damages trust and can get you penalised.

## 6. Contact details that match everywhere

Your business name, phone number and address should be exactly the same on your website, Google Business Profile, Instagram, Justdial and other listings. Consistency helps both Google and AI tools trust that you are a real, local business.

## 7. Basic SEO done properly

Every page needs a unique title and meta description, one clear H1 heading, descriptive image alt text, a sitemap and a connection to Google Search Console. These basics are free and make a real difference.

## 8. Structured data (schema)

Schema markup is code that tells search engines what your page is about: your organisation, services, FAQs and articles. It can earn richer search results and helps AI answer engines understand and quote your business correctly.

## 9. Answers to the questions customers actually ask

Think about what people ask before they buy: price factors, timelines, what's included. Answer those questions plainly on your pages. Direct, factual answers are exactly what Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity look for.

## 10. Simple enquiry forms

Ask only for what you need: name, what they want, and how to reach them. Every extra field loses people. Send the enquiry straight to WhatsApp or email, and save it so nothing gets lost.

## 11. Tracking set up before you run ads

Install Google Analytics 4, and set up Google Ads and Meta Pixel conversion tracking for WhatsApp clicks and form submissions before spending on ads. Without it, you can't tell which ads bring customers.

## 12. Fresh content, regularly

A site that never changes gives search engines little reason to come back. A regular blog that answers customer questions builds visibility month after month, and gives you useful posts to share on social media.
